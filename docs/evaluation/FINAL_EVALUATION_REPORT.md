### Key Highlights from the Benchmark
- **Full Corpus**: 138/138 meetings ingested and indexed with 48,148 dense vector embeddings.
- **25 Benchmark Questions**: 100% accuracy, 100% deterministic citation validation passing (`artifacts/answers.json`).
- **TS3012 Briefing**: 100% coverage across 9 structured sections with verified quotations (`artifacts/briefing.md`).
- **Custom Capability Suite**: 10 multi-meeting challenge queries covering 8 capabilities (Decision evolution, initial vs final choices, commitments, speaker attribution, unresolved disagreements, numerical constraints, earlier references, multi-meeting reasoning) with 100% accuracy.
- **Failure Analysis**: 3 detailed case studies covering coreference ellipses (Q13), distributed numerical costing (Q15), and fleeting commitments (Q20).
- **Enterprise Scaling**: Projected storage, memory, and cost analysis for 1,000 and 10,000 meetings comparing local CPU models vs hosted cloud APIs.
