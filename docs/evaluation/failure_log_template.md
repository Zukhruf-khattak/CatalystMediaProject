# Failure Log Template

## Mandatory Deliverable
The assessment requires documenting at least three genuine failure cases encountered during development or evaluation.

---

### Failure Case Template
```markdown
### Failure [ID]: [Short Title]
- **Target Question / Query**:
- **Target Team & Meetings**:
- **Expected Outcome & Ground Truth**:
- **Agent's Generated Output**:
- **Failure Classification**: [Retrieval Miss | Temporal Inversion | Speech Disfluency Trap | Citation Validation Failure]
- **Root Cause Analysis**:
  - Why did the agent fail?
  - Did the model misread unfinished sentences or filler words?
  - Did lexical search fail due to synonym divergence?
- **Remediation / Architectural Fix**:
```
